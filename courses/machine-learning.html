Key Takeaway: Monotonic decrease to a local minimum

Both K-Means steps strictly non-increase the Within-Cluster Sum of Squares (SSE): assigning points to the nearest centroid minimizes distance for fixed centers, and moving each centroid to the arithmetic mean minimizes squared distance for fixed assignments. Try clicking Init Centroids multiple times to see how bad initial seeds can trap K-Means in suboptimal local minima.
